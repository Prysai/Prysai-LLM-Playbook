# Sources, archives, and assets

This directory records what came from outside the project and what may be
published. The [asset register](asset-register.md) is the first stop for any
borrowed structure, research source, image, archive, or third-party method.

## Required record

Before adapting external material, record its source, revision or access date,
license signal, attribution, intended scope, and distribution decision. If the
boundary is unclear, keep the material reference-only and write an original
explanation.

- [Asset and source register](asset-register.md)
- [Archive audit](archive-audit-2026-08-09.json)
- [Skill integration decisions](skill-integration-decisions.md)
- [Skill candidate catalog](skill-candidate-catalog.md)

Research links are not a license to copy. A repository-level license may not
cover its images, fonts, submissions, external services, or nested material.
