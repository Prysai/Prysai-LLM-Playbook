## Evidence levels and review rules

- A `user report` proves only that the reporter says they observed the symptom in the stated environment; testing across environments does not automatically make it official confirmation.
- An `answerer suggestion` records a community workaround and its risks; it does not become current configuration syntax or support policy by itself.
- `Official confirmation` requires a maintainer reply, official documentation, a fix record, or a checkable release note; an automated deduplication bot does not count.
- `Local reproduction` can be filled only after this project runs the case and saves evidence; this index currently has no local reproduction.
- Whenever a volatile case is cited in the book, retain the original URL, issue status, reported version/platform, access date, and the “not reproduced in this project” boundary. `closed` describes the page state, not a fix or availability for every account.
