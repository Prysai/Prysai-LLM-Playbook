# Six messages for a truthful work-update practice loop

Use fictional facts only. This is a text-only practice loop for making one short
update easier to inspect. It does not establish that an update is accurate,
professional, approved, or suitable for real work. Stop if the task needs
private, customer, employment, financial, or production information.

## 1. Set the reader and the facts

```text
I want to practise a short fictional work update. Ask me for one reader, up to
three confirmed facts, one unknown or blocker, and one possible ask. Do not
invent progress, dates, causes, approvals, or promises. Return only a compact
fact list and wait for my own draft.
```

## 2. Write before seeing a model version

```text
Using only the fictional facts we saved, ask me to write a 60-word update for
the named reader. Do not draft it, improve it, or add a subject line. Preserve
my first attempt exactly after I send it.
```

## 3. Find one material gap

```text
Compare my first attempt with this check: the reader can find the confirmed
facts, the unknown or blocker, and any ask; no unsupported progress or promise
appears. Identify at most one material gap. Quote the relevant words. If the
facts do not decide it, say "unknown". Do not rewrite my update.
```

## 4. Revise in my own words

```text
Give one partial hint for that single gap, then wait for my revision. Do not
write a replacement update unless I explicitly ask after trying. Keep my first
attempt, your hint, and my revision separate.
```

## 5. Change the audience

```text
Keep exactly the same fictional facts, but change the reader from a teammate to
a manager. Ask for a new update without hints. Then label what changed in tone,
detail, and ask. Do not turn an unknown into a fact or say either version is
approved.
```

## 6. Leave a small receipt

```text
Return a practice receipt: fictional facts | first attempt | one hint | learner
revision | changed-audience attempt | additions to check | unknowns. End with
exactly one status: template_selected, practised, not_run, or blocked. Do not
claim better productivity, better writing, workplace readiness, or retention.
```

Keep the receipt with the two drafts. One recorded loop is practice, not an
independent assessment or proof that an AI's feedback was correct.
