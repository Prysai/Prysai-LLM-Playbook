# Community conduct

Prysai LLM Playbook is a learning project maintained by Prysai Lab. We want
technical disagreement, correction, and careful scepticism; we do not want
people to be attacked for asking basic questions, reporting a mistake, or
declining a request that lacks evidence or authority.

## Expected participation

- Discuss the work, evidence, and scope rather than making claims about a
  person or group.
- State uncertainty, limits, and conflicts of interest plainly.
- Respect a contributor's decision not to share private data, credentials,
  unpublished work, model transcripts, or employer material.
- Keep feedback specific enough that a maintainer can inspect it: identify the
  path, claim, reproduction boundary, or proposed small correction.
- Leave space for beginners. A request for a definition, a smaller example, or
  a safer stopping point is a valid contribution to the curriculum.

## Unacceptable participation

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, threats, or sexualized language.
- Publishing personal data, credentials, private messages, customer material,
  raw participant records, or private repositories without permission.
- Pressuring someone to bypass a stated safety boundary, license restriction,
  review gate, or stop condition.
- Passing copied, generated, or unverified material off as original evidence
  or an independently reviewed result.

## Maintainer response

Prysai Lab may ask for a correction, edit or hide a contribution, close an
interaction, or restrict future participation when this policy is breached.
Responses will be limited to the material needed to make a safe repository
decision. This policy does not promise a response time, confidentiality beyond
what the reporting channel can provide, a particular outcome, or a public
explanation of a private report.

## Report privately when possible

Do not put a conduct incident, personal data, or private evidence in a public
issue, pull request, screenshot, or field report. Use an already authorized
private channel with Prysai Lab and share the smallest useful description:
the repository surface, what happened, any immediate safety concern, and the
outcome you need. Remove credentials and unnecessary personal details first.

If the report concerns a vulnerability, credential exposure, workflow
compromise, or another security risk, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead. If no private channel has been established for a non-security report,
do not publish incident details merely to obtain one; make only a neutral,
detail-free request for a private reporting route.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and project-maintained learning or research activities. It is a candidate
community policy for this repository. It does not prove that an external
community, moderation system, or independent reporting process already
exists.
